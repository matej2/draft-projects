Everything you need to feel in control of your money

Know your spending

Add your expenses in seconds, or bring in your past ones all at once from a spreadsheet. Each expense is sorted into categories like Groceries, Leisure, Health, or Utilities, so you can see at a glance what you spend and where. Whether you pay daily, weekly, monthly, or once a year, Budget Lens keeps track and shows you the full picture.

Stay in control

Set a budget for any category and always know how much you’ve used and how much you have left. Look back over the past week, month, or three months, or pick your own dates.

Reach your goals

Saving for a trip, a new laptop, or a rainy day? Set a savings goal and watch your progress.

Get smart, friendly advice

Every week, Budget Lens looks for anything unusual in your spending, like a category that suddenly costs much more than normal. If it finds something, it explains what happened in plain language and suggests practical ways to cut back.

Look ahead

See which costs repeat and which are one-off. Budget Lens estimates what’s coming next, so there are fewer surprises at the end of the month. It also helps you spot purchases you could trim or drop.

Private and secure

Your account is protected, and your financial details stay yours.
